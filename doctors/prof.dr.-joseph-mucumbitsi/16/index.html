Prof.Dr. Joseph MUCUMBITSI

Prof. Dr. Joseph Mucumbitsi—a pioneer of pediatric cardiology in Rwanda and former head of cardiology at the University Teaching Hospital of Kigali—now brings his three decades of expertise to Deva Medical Center, where he heads the Pediatric Cardiology service. A founding member of the Rwanda Heart Foundation, he couples hands‑on care for children with congenital and acquired heart disease with a strong commitment to research, early‑screening advocacy, and clinician training. Through international collaborations and local outreach programs, Prof. Dr. Mucumbitsi is helping Deva Medical Center set new standards in cardiovascular care for patients across Rwanda and the wider region.
